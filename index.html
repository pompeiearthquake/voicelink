Terms of Service & Privacy Policy

VoiceLink · Student Council · Last updated November 2026

1. What VoiceLink is

VoiceLink is a moderated wall where students share opinions, suggestions and concerns with the student council. Each grade and section has its own wall. By creating an account or using VoiceLink, you agree to these terms.

2. Data collection

We keep only what's needed to run your account:

  • Your display name, grade and section, and a username if you choose to set one.
  • Your password, saved only as a one-way hash (see Security standards). The password itself is never stored.
  • A private record of each time an admin, or a council member an admin has authorized, looks up who wrote an anonymous post (who looked, which post, and when).

Plus what you create while using the wall: your posts, comments, stars and saved posts, with the times they were made. Posts that are archived stay stored, with your real name, until an admin or council member permanently deletes them. A login cookie keeps you signed in for up to 7 days. We don't collect your email, phone number, birthday or location, and there is no advertising or tracking.

One thing to know: this page loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google to download them.

3. Security standards

  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt, a one-way method. Hashes can't be turned back into passwords, so nobody, including admins, can read yours. Forgotten passwords can't be looked up.
  • Account data is kept in a private database on a persistent storage volume attached to the server. It is not publicly downloadable.
  • You stay signed in with a secure, HTTP-only cookie. Login, sign-up and profile changes are rate-limited to block guessing attacks.
  • Names, posts and comments are shown as plain text, never as code, and the server checks which section each post belongs to before showing it to you.

No online system can promise to be perfectly secure, so please pick a strong password that you don't use anywhere else.

4. Who sees your data and how it's used

Your posts and comments are visible to logged-in students in your grade and section, and to admins and council members, who can switch between every section’s wall. Posts written by admins or council members, and the pinned post, are visible to every logged-in student, but comments on them stay limited to the section they were posted for.

Anonymous posts. When you post anonymously, other students see “Anonymous” instead of your name. It does not hide you from the school’s administrators: admins (advisers) can always see the real name behind an anonymous post, so they can deal with abuse. Student council members can only see it if an admin has explicitly authorized them, and every lookup is recorded. The archive, which only admins and council members can open, shows the real author of an anonymous post to those same people only.

Your data is used only to run VoiceLink: logging you in, showing your section's wall and moderating it. It is for internal student council use. It is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with outside parties.

5. Code of conduct

  • Be respectful. No harassment, bullying, abuse, threats, hate speech or discrimination.
  • No profanity or slurs, in English or Filipino. VoiceLink automatically blocks posts, comments and names that contain them, including disguised spellings.
  • Don't share anyone's private information (addresses, phone numbers, photos, passwords) and don't pretend to be someone else.
  • Keep it constructive and on topic: suggestions, concerns and feedback about school life. No spam or advertising.
  • Use one account, keep your password to yourself, and remember you're responsible for what's posted under your account. Anonymous posts follow the same rules.

6. Moderation

Admins and council members may, at their discretion and without notice: archive any post (it disappears from the wall and moves to a private archive), delete comments, pin posts, mark posts as In Review, Planned or Resolved, permanently delete archived posts, and restrict or remove accounts that break these rules. Only admins and council members can archive posts, view the archive or permanently delete posts. Section presidents and students cannot. Serious violations may be passed to school staff under the school's own rules.

Council members, admins and section presidents can create main posts. Every logged-in student can read their own section's wall, comment, star and save posts.

7. Your choices

You can change your display name and username with Edit profile, and your password from the side menu, any time. Changing your password signs you out of every device. To have a post, comment or your whole account removed, ask a council member.

8. Changes

These terms may be updated as VoiceLink changes. Continuing to use VoiceLink after an update means you accept the new version.
